# Gallery: finished Toast pages

Each folder is one page drawn by the real renderer in the light look, with the data and the page patch that made it.
Open the picture first. Use these as the picture to beat (SKILL.md step 3) when the person gave no reference: not
to copy their content, but to hold your result to the same finish.

| Case | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `spending-page/` | A reading-width page. The headline states the finding (spent, and against last month); the purchases lead with the shop's photos; amounts are short and right-aligned; the rest folds under "N more". No chart: the headline already answers the comparison. |
| `status-page/` | A plan with one step waiting on the person. That step leads as a band with a bell, saying what to do and why now; under it the headline states where things stand, one split bar, then every step with colour only on the one that is the person's turn and the one in motion. |

Each `note.md` holds the layout note; `page.yaml` is the page (blocks, spreads, slots); `value.json` is its data.
